FAQ

Answers before
you order.

Quick answers about garments, decoration, artwork, minimums, rush service, uniforms and payment.

What can I order online?

The initial guided custom catalog is intentionally focused on T-shirts, polos and hats. Advanced can still handle other products through a direct custom quote.

Do I need to know a SanMar product number?

No. The new order flow presents Advanced's selected garments with customer-friendly names, real product imagery, colors and sizes. Supplier style numbers stay behind the scenes.

Which decoration methods are available?

Advanced offers embroidery, screen printing and DTF. Available choices can vary by garment and placement so the ordering system only shows compatible options.

How do rush orders work?

When production capacity allows, jobs requested 15–10 days out add a $50 rush charge; eligible jobs under 10 days add $100. High-volume seasons may require more time.

How do Espirito Santo uniforms work?

The school catalog is predetermined. Approved tops include the Espirito Santo logo on the left chest unless otherwise noted. Customers choose sizes and quantities instead of designing the garment.

How is payment processed?

Online orders are paid through Advanced's Square account. PrintFlow creates the verified order and then sends the customer to secure Square checkout.

Can I bring my own garments?

Advanced may accept customer-supplied garments depending on the project. Those jobs should be reviewed directly rather than sent through the instant online catalog.

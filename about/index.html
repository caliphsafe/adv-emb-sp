About

Built through skill, service and repeat customers.

Advanced Embroidery has served businesses, schools, organizations and individuals from New Bedford since 2003.

Our story

A small team with a careful approach.

Maria Pimentel founded Advanced Embroidery in 2003, bringing years of industry experience and a commitment to quality work.

The business grew through word of mouth, long-term relationships and a belief that strong craftsmanship and clear service should go together. Today, the team handles everything from one-piece personalization to larger custom apparel and uniform orders.

Our promise:
We review the details, communicate the plan and treat every order as work that represents both your name and ours.

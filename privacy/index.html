Privacy

How website inquiries and order requests are handled.

This policy explains the information submitted through this website and how it is used.

Information collected

When you submit a contact, custom order or uniform request, Advanced Embroidery may receive your name, contact information, organization, project details, artwork and any other information you choose to provide.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, confirm orders, provide customer service and maintain records related to the requested work.

Artwork and uploaded files

Artwork is used only to evaluate or produce the requested project unless a separate permission is provided. Do not upload files containing sensitive personal information.

Third-party services

The website may use hosting, email delivery, analytics or mapping services. Those providers may process limited technical data required to operate their services.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to mpimentel1363@gmail.com or discussed by calling 508-678-8993.

How It Works

A clear path from project idea to finished apparel.

You can arrive with complete artwork and product numbers—or simply describe what you are trying to create.

Submit your project

Tell us what you are ordering, approximately how many pieces you need, your target date and whether you already have artwork.

Review products and method

Browse SanMar or ask for recommendations. We determine whether embroidery, screen printing or DTF is the best fit.

Confirm quote and artwork

The team confirms pricing, colors, placement, size range and any digitizing or artwork preparation required.

Approve and produce

Once the order details are approved, production begins. Completed orders are quality checked before pickup or delivery.

What helps us quote faster

Share what you know.

  • Apparel types and approximate quantities
  • Preferred colors and size range
  • Logo or artwork file
  • Desired print or embroidery locations
  • Target completion date
  • Any product numbers you already selected
What can remain undecided

We can help with the rest.

  • Which production method to choose
  • Which garment brand or style fits best
  • Thread or ink color matching
  • Artwork preparation and digitizing
  • Placement and size recommendations
  • How to organize a group order

Rush timing: Timing is reviewed before an order is confirmed. Requests needed 10–15 days from submission carry a $50 rush charge; requests needed in fewer than 10 days carry a $100 rush charge and remain subject to production availability.

Tell us what you need, upload your artwork if you have it, and our team will follow up with pricing and next steps.
